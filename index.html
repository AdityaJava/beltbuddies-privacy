Privacy Policy: Belt Buddies

Effective date: 30 October 2026 (updated the same day for ads and purchases)

Belt Buddies: Color Shooter ("the game") is developed by Aditya Dudhal. This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and how you can control it.

Data we collect and why

  • Gameplay and app-usage data, through Google Analytics for Firebase: levels started, won and failed, how far through a level you got, how long a level took, boosters used, tutorial steps, the theme you chose, and session counts. We use it to find levels that are too hard, fix the tutorial, and improve the game.
  • Device and app information that Firebase collects automatically, such as device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country (from the IP address, which is not stored), and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase.
  • Crash reports, through Firebase Crashlytics: the technical state of the app when it crashes (stack trace, device model, Android version), used only to fix bugs.
  • Game settings, through Firebase Remote Config: the game downloads tuning values (for example game speed). This sends the app-instance identifier, not personal data.
  • Ads, through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK): the game can show optional video ads that you choose to watch for a reward (for example double coins or an extra life), and short ads between levels after you win, from level 20 on. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob collects and processes the advertising identifier of your device, approximate location derived from the IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information. Players who buy "No Ads" see no ads between levels.
  • Purchases, through Google Play Billing: the game sells optional coin packs, a "No Ads" upgrade and special offers. Google Play handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game records which item was bought and its price in Google Analytics for Firebase, to understand which offers players find useful.

What we do NOT collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or account login.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase does not collect the advertising identifier; only the ads SDK uses it, to show and measure ads.
  • No payment details: Google Play processes every purchase.

Your progress (levels, lives, coins, boosters, purchases, settings) is stored only on your device.

Your choices

You can turn off Usage data at any time in the game: open Settings (the gear on the map) and set Usage data to Off. The game then stops sending analytics events. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes everything stored on your device.

Ad consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game sends one reminder when your lives are full again. You can turn this off in your Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Firebase privacy and security
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config: same policy as above
  • Google AdMob: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob partners
  • Google Play Billing: Google Payments privacy notice
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Data retention and how to request deletion

Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, under Google's policies. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your device:

  1. Email adityadudhal97@gmail.com with the subject line "Belt Buddies Data Deletion Request".
  2. Tell us roughly when you played and on which device model, so we can find the data.
  3. We will process the request and confirm by email within 30 days.

Security

All data the game sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and handled by Google's infrastructure.
